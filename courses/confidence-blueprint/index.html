Questions

Frequently asked

Is this therapy? ▼
No. This course draws on evidence-based techniques from CBT and exposure therapy, but it is not a substitute for professional mental health treatment. If your social anxiety is severe or significantly impacting your daily life, we encourage you to also work with a therapist.
How severe does my anxiety need to be? ▼
The course works for a wide spectrum — from mild social self-consciousness to more significant anxiety that leads to consistent avoidance. It's not designed for clinical social anxiety disorder requiring medical management.
I've tried things before and nothing worked. Why would this be different? ▼
Most approaches either stay entirely theoretical or jump to action too fast. This course maps your specific anxiety pattern first, then builds exposure in a graded, systematic way — at a pace you control. The tools provide objective data so you're not just relying on self-perception.
Do I need to buy the Humanly Labs tools separately? ▼
No. The course price includes access to all three Humanly Labs tools used in the lessons.
What if I'm also introverted, not just anxious? ▼
The course distinguishes clearly between introversion (a personality trait) and social anxiety (a fear response). You're not trying to become an extrovert — you're trying to remove the fear so that connecting on your own terms feels possible and enjoyable.
