Questions

Frequently asked

How long do the lessons take? ▼
Each lesson is designed to take 20–40 minutes to read and complete. The full course is built to run over 90 days, but you can move faster or slower — it's entirely self-paced.
I moved a year ago — is it too late? ▼
Not at all. The strategies apply whether you moved last week or two years ago. Many students join after months of struggling alone — the course is designed to work from wherever you're starting.
Do I need to buy the Humanly Labs tools separately? ▼
The course price includes access to all six Humanly Labs tools referenced in the lessons. You won't be asked to purchase anything else.
I'm introverted. Will this work for me? ▼
Yes — the course is specifically designed to accommodate a range of social personalities. Lesson 1.2 (Your Social Connection Profile) uses the Loneliness Type Quiz to personalise the approach to your specific needs from the very start.
What's your refund policy? ▼
If you complete the first two modules within 30 days of purchase and feel the course isn't right for you, email us and we'll refund in full. We want the course to earn its place in your life.
