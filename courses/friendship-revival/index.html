Questions

Frequently asked

What if I genuinely have no friends to revive? ▼
The course covers both reviving dormant connections and building new ones from scratch. Module 5 is specifically focused on forming new adult friendships when you don't have a base to rebuild from.
I'm in my 40s/50s. Is it too late? ▼
No. Friendship formation in your 40s and 50s is different from your 20s — but it's absolutely possible. The course accounts for the realities of later-life social rebuilding: less free time, stronger preferences, more responsibility. These aren't obstacles, they're parameters to work with.
What if I'm not sure my old friendships are worth reviving? ▼
Lesson 3.3 (Reviving vs Releasing) deals with exactly this. Not every dormant friendship deserves effort — the course helps you figure out which ones do, and gives you permission to let the others go without guilt.
Do I need to buy the Humanly Labs tools separately? ▼
No. The course price includes access to all three Humanly Labs tools used in the lessons: the Friendship Audit, Check-In Generator, and Attachment Style Quiz.
My partner or spouse is my only close relationship. Is this course for me? ▼
Yes — this is one of the most common patterns the course is designed for. Relying on one person for all your social and emotional needs puts enormous pressure on that relationship. The course helps you build a broader support network while protecting what you already have.
